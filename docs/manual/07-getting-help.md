## Shake to report

Something broken, or an idea? **Shake your phone**, or the kitchen iPad, on any page, and the reporter opens.

- On an iPhone or iPad, the app first asks to use motion: the reporter asks on your first tap after a page opens. If you said no, turn it on again in **Settings → Bugs and feature requests → Turn on shake to report**.
- The same Settings card has **Report a bug** and **Request a feature**, if you would rather tap than shake.
- On the kitchen iPad, tap your avatar first, so the report is from you.

## What a report sends

A report becomes an issue on the household's **public** GitHub tracker, so anyone on the internet can read it.

- It never carries your name or email, and the app removes things that look like personal data (emails, phone numbers, codes and tokens) before it is sent. That removal works on patterns and can miss things, so do not write names or anything private.
- **Improve with AI** can tidy your words before it is filed.
- Device details and recent errors go only if you tick the box, and the reporter shows exactly what they are first.

## When something breaks

If a page hits an error, a small notice offers **Report this bug** (or **Not now**). A page that fails completely offers Report, Try again and Home.

## Asking a person

Questions about the house, the points or your data go to the owner, in the house or in the house's Telegram group.
