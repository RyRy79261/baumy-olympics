The full, exact answer is the [privacy page](/privacy). This is the short version, with a link to each part.

## What the app keeps about you

Your account (email, name, how you sign in), your member profile (display name, colour, character, your PIN only as a scrambled hash, and your Telegram id if you link it), and what you do in the game and on the board: chores and disputes, notes and reminders. There is also an audit log of who changed what, and when. See [what we keep](/privacy#what-we-keep).

## Where it lives, and who else sees it

The database is in Frankfurt, in the EU. Some services the app uses are outside it:

- when you ask Baumy something, Anthropic (the AI) gets your words and what the app looks up to answer;
- when you hold the microphone, Groq turns the clip into text;
- Google Calendar holds the house calendar, and Baumy Brain and Telegram see what goes through Telegram.

See [where it lives](/privacy#where-it-lives) and [who else sees what](/privacy#who-else-sees-what). There are no ads, no tracking and no selling: [no ads, no selling, no tracking](/privacy#no-ads-no-selling-no-tracking).

The app counts how much each member uses Baumy, but does not keep what you said.

## How long it is kept

Proof photos are deleted {{PHOTO_RETENTION_DAYS}} days after their claim settled. Most other things stay while the household uses the app, including the audit log and deleted notes. See [how long we keep it](/privacy#how-long-we-keep-it), and the [cookies](/privacy#cookies) the app sets.

## Seeing, changing or deleting it

To see what the app keeps about you, open **Settings → Your data**: counts and dates (your sign-in methods, devices, completions, notes, proof photos and when each is deleted, audit-log entries, Baumy usage and connected apps), with how long each is kept. Or ask Baumy "what do you keep about me?" on your own phone or computer; it never shows this on the kitchen iPad, and it only ever covers you.

You can change your name, colour and character yourself in **Settings**. For a full copy of what the app holds about you, or to have something removed, ask the owner. See [seeing, changing and deleting your data](/privacy#seeing-changing-and-deleting-your-data).

## The house rules

The [terms](/terms) are short: log the chores you really did, keep notes kind and free of secrets, keep your password and PIN to yourself. See [be decent](/terms#be-decent).
